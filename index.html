Hi, welcome to my personal web page!

I'm Damien Remot, and here you can find a summary of my work, my current projects and my background ^^

I earned a PhD in applied mathematics in 2025. My thesis focused on active sampling and surrogate modeling for expensive simulators, in collaboration with NXP Semiconductors and ANITI.

My work sits between probability, statistics and machine learning, with a taste for turning methods into code that actually runs! That is how AIMS came about, an open-source Python project for adaptive and iterative model sampling, based on the work done during my PhD.

I now teach mathematics and I am preparing the agrégation, the French competitive teaching examination.

Alongside, I keep coding, both software projects to keep learning (web development, for instance) and scientific code at the crossroads of statistics, sampling and machine learning.
